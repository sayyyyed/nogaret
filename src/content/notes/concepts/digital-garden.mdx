A digital garden is a personal knowledge base published as a website, where ideas grow organically over time instead of being finalized in a single post.

## Core ideas

- **Evergreen notes** are written to be durable and revisited, not timestamped and forgotten.
- **Maps of content (MOCs)** act as hubs that connect related notes into navigable clusters.
- **Atomicity** means each note covers one idea well enough to stand alone.
- **Bi-directional links** let knowledge surface connections between notes automatically.

## Why publish it

Publishing forces clarity, keeps notes accountable, and lets readers follow the same paths you walked.

## Growing a garden

Start small: one permanent note per useful idea, one reference note per useful source, and one MOC to tie them together. Let the structure emerge from what you actually use.
